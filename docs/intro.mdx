# AI Hedge Documentation

AI Hedge is a **decentralized, non-custodial yield optimization protocol** — combining AI-driven capital allocation, regime-aware market strategies, and a battle-tested multi-strategy vault architecture built on the EVM.

---

## What is AI Hedge?

AI Hedge is an automated yield routing and compounding layer that maximizes risk-adjusted returns for depositors in a fully permissionless, trustless manner. It operates across four interconnected pillars:

| Pillar | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **The Protocol** | Immutable EVM smart contracts built on the ERC-4626 multi-strategy vault standard |
| **The Interfaces** | The official dashboard at [dapp.aihedge.finance](https://dapp.aihedge.finance) and any third-party integrations |
| **Governance** | Multi-sig security committee and quantitative vault curation framework, with progressive decentralization planned as the protocol scales |
| **Contributors** | ML researchers, smart contract engineers, and community members building and auditing the protocol |

---

## Core Architecture

AI Hedge vaults are **ERC-4626 compliant** — a battle-tested standard ensuring:

- 🔐 **Self-custody**: Depositors always hold their own vault share tokens
- ⚡ **Composability**: Any DApp, smart contract, or institutional portal can integrate natively
- ♻️ **Permissionless compounding**: Strategies run autonomously without centralized operators

## Product Offerings & Comparison

AI Hedge organizes its decentralized finance products into three specialized tiers:

| Product Tier | Supported Networks | Underlying Strategies | Best Suited For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Multi-Strategy Yield Vaults** | Ethereum Mainnet, Base | Lending Markets, Concentrated Liquidity, Market Making, Auto-Compounding | Depositors seeking actively rebalanced, multi-pool yield optimization with risk-controlled diversification. |
| **Flexible Earn (Savings)** | Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, BNB Chain | Direct Money Market Lending | Conservative depositors seeking direct interest on single stablecoins with zero lockup. |
| **Liquid Staking** | Ethereum Mainnet | Proof-of-Stake Consensus Staking | ETH holders looking to earn base consensus staking yield while preserving liquidity across DeFi. |

---

## Key Capabilities

- **AI-Powered Allocation** — Reinforcement learning (PPO) models analyze yield opportunities, gas costs, and liquidity depth in real time to optimize capital routing across strategies
- **Curated Multi-Strategy Vaults** — Vault curators configure asset distributions, risk guardrails, and strategy whitelists to match specific risk profiles
- **Flexible Savings & Staking** — Seamless aggregation of stablecoin savings products with Ethereum liquid staking strategies
- **1-Click Cross-Chain Deposits** — Deposit any token from any supported blockchain directly into target vaults with automated swap and bridge routing
- **Black-Swan Circuit Breakers** — Automated risk controls including volatility-triggered de-risking, multi-oracle validation, and emergency withdrawal paths
- **Open Integration** — Any protocol, aggregator, or institution can plug into AI Hedge vaults via the standard ERC-4626 interface
