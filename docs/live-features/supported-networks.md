## 1-Click Cross-Chain Deposits

Have funds on a different network than your target vault? **You don't need to manually bridge or swap beforehand.**

AI Hedge features built-in **1-Click Cross-Chain Routing** supporting deposits from:

* **Ethereum**
* **Base**
* **Arbitrum One**
* **BNB Chain**
* **Optimism**
* **Polygon**

### How it works for you:
1. Open the deposit tab on any vault.
2. Select your source network and the token you hold (e.g. USDT on Arbitrum).
3. Confirm the deposit — AI Hedge handles the swap and bridge routing behind the scenes in a single transaction.
4. You only need gas on your source chain. No destination network gas tokens required!

## Supported Wallets

AI Hedge supports standard Web3 wallet connections with full mobile and desktop compatibility:

### 1. Browser Wallets (Extensions)
If you use a desktop browser extension, the DApp automatically detects it for instant 1-click connection:
- **MetaMask**
- **OKX Wallet**
- **Coinbase Wallet**
- **Rabby Wallet**
- **Brave Wallet** & other injected Web3 wallets

### 2. WalletConnect (Mobile & Hardware)
If you prefer managing your funds on a smartphone or hardware device, use **WalletConnect**:
- **Mobile Wallets:** Trust Wallet, Rainbow, Safe, imToken, Bitget Wallet, and 300+ others.
- **How to connect:** Click **Connect Wallet** &rarr; select **WalletConnect** &rarr; scan the on-screen QR code with your mobile wallet app.
- **Hardware Wallets:** Securely sign transactions via Ledger or Trezor connected through your mobile or browser wallet.

## Automatic Network Switching

If your wallet is currently set to a different network than the vault you are viewing, there is no need to manually change settings in your wallet:

1. The action button in the DApp will automatically say **"Switch to [Network]"** (e.g., *Switch to Base*).
2. Click the button.
3. Your wallet will prompt you to approve switching chains — click **Approve** to switch immediately.
