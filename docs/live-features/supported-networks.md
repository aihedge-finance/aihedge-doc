# Supported Networks & Wallets

The AI Hedge protocol and DApp interface support multiple EVM networks with built-in cross-chain routing, allowing you to deposit funds seamlessly from any supported chain.

---

## Supported Networks

You can deposit assets, bridge, and run strategies across these networks:

* **Ethereum Mainnet (ETH Main)**
* **BNB Chain**
* **Arbitrum**
* **Base**
* **Optimism**

> [!TIP]
> **Cross-Chain Deposits**: You don't need to manually bridge funds before depositing. Select your source asset on any connected chain in the deposit module to swap, bridge, and enter vaults in a single transaction.

---

## Supported Wallets

Connect your secure Web3 wallet to interact with the platform:

* **WalletConnect** (Mobile & Desktop)
* **OKX Wallet** (Browser Extension & Mobile)
* **MetaMask** (Browser Extension & Mobile)
