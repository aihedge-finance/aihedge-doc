# Product Explanation & Proprietary Know-How Protection

Guidelines for explaining AI Hedge products, protecting proprietary intellectual property, and maintaining accessible communication standards.

---

## 1. Zero Know-How Leakage
- **Protect Algorithmic Know-How & Alpha**: When explaining AI Hedge products, architecture, or features, **never** disclose proprietary trading algorithms, internal predictive signals, exact model formulas, quantitative parameters, or execution logic.
- **Benefit-Oriented Descriptions**: Focus on the outcomes and user benefits (e.g., automated risk-adjusted yields, real-time capital protection, non-custodial vault safety) rather than the internal algorithmic machinery.

---

## 2. Terminology Substitutions
- **No "LP Provider"**: Do not use "LP Provider" or "Liquidity Provider" when explaining protocol mechanics or product features.
- **Use "Market Making"**: Always use **Market Making** (or **Market Making Strategy**) as the standard substitute term.

---

## 3. Layman Terms for Quantitative Modules (Product Info)
In **Product Info**, overview documents, feature cards, and general user-facing descriptions, replace deep mathematical and quantitative engineering terms with intuitive layman language:

| Module / Topic | Prohibited / Overly Technical Jargon | Approved Layman Term |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Liquidity Operations** | LP Provider, Liquidity Provider | **Market Making**, **Market Making Strategy** |
| **Risk Management** | Parametric CVaR, covariance matrices, liquidation boundary vectors | **Capital Protection**, **Safety Guardrails**, **Automated Risk Shield**, **Loss Prevention** |
| **Optimization Engine** | Convex quadratic programming, solver iterations, heuristic weight tensors | **Smart Capital Allocation**, **Dynamic Yield Rebalancing**, **Intelligent Allocation Engine** |
| **Execution Mechanics** | Micro-rebalance loops, MEV capture routing | **Automated Execution**, **Optimal Route Execution** |
